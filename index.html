About Us

About Us

J.florie is committed to designing exquisite bouquets for all
occasions and days. Because each handmade bouquet is
thoughtfully chosen to meet the preferences and requirements of
the recipient, each one is unique and has it own individually and
character.


J.florie was founded in 2022, and our goal is to go spreading
love through flowers and enhancing everyone's life with
happiness, beauty, and creativity. Even while flowers are lovely in
and of themselves, they require skilled hands to bring out their full
beauty.
